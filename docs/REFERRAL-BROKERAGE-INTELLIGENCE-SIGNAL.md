# Referral Brokerage Intelligence Signal

Referral brokerage is a useful MONY revenue model: connect a verified buyer with a verified provider and earn a pre-agreed commission when the contractual trigger occurs.

Current market evidence shows multiple active models: agency referral programs, B2B referral agreements, and marketplace affiliate programs. Examples include agency programs paying commissions on collected revenue and marketplace programs paying first-order plus recurring revenue share. These examples are evidence that the mechanism exists, not a guarantee of earnings.

## Extracted capability

The valuable pattern is a closed attribution loop:

buyer need → provider offer → referral registration → protected attribution window → deal → collected payment → commission reconciliation.

## MONY use

Treat this as a separate revenue lane from affiliate links. It can monetize high-value B2B services such as web development, automation, AI implementation, marketing, design and other services where a provider explicitly offers referral compensation.

## Failure modes

- provider does not pay
- attribution dispute
- duplicate lead
- unqualified buyer
- provider cannot fulfil
- commission terms change
- refund/chargeback
- privacy/marketing-law violation
- unlicensed brokerage activity in a regulated sector

## Decision

KEEP. Build as a controlled referral-brokerage capability, not as uncontrolled lead scraping or spam.
