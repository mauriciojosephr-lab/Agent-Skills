# Value Packaging Intelligence Signal

Packaging can change perceived value and willingness to pay, but the effect is contextual rather than a guaranteed markup. Research has found that packaging interaction can change willingness to pay, and recent empirical work links perceived packaging design with premium willingness in specific product/cultural contexts. ## Extracted capability

The reusable capability is **Perceived-Value Engineering**:

product truth → positioning → brand signal → packaging → story → visual proof → unboxing → offer → experiment → measured outcome.

The system should not assume that a 50→250 price jump is caused by packaging alone. It should test the contribution of each layer and compare incremental margin, not just selling price.

## Integration

- EASY: combine with Product DNA/Product Integrity and the creative engine to help sellers turn ordinary products into coherent, differentiated offers while preserving factual product identity.
- MONY: offer packaging/value-presentation as a service lane and use it to create conversion-tested product creatives and landing pages.
- Elite/ARMY-14: treat perceived-value claims as hypotheses requiring measurable evidence.

## Guardrails

No fake scarcity, fabricated origin/story, false quality claims, altered product specifications, deceptive before/after imagery, or unsupported sustainability claims.

## Decision

KEEP. Build as a controlled capability and experimentation layer, not as a promise that design automatically multiplies price.
