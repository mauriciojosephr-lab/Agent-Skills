## MONY execution rule
Do not build 50 separate businesses.

catalog → cluster → capability reuse → offer → market test → evidence → scale/kill

Upwork's August 2026 marketplace report identified AI automation as its most-searched AI term and reported AI video generation among skills growing both year-over-year and month-over-month. Its 2026 research also reports price pressure on lower-complexity AI execution while more complex AI-augmented work gained value. These are market signals, not guarantees of profitability.
