# Prior Art to Invention
1. Collect only relevant prior art.
2. Extract mechanisms, strengths, weaknesses, and gaps.
3. Identify what existing approaches cannot do or do poorly.
4. Design a differentiated mechanism that closes one or more gaps.
5. Build a minimal concrete version.
6. Compare it against the prior art.
7. Preserve attribution/provenance where required and do not copy protected content unnecessarily.

The deliverable must contain an original improvement or composition, not just a research summary.
