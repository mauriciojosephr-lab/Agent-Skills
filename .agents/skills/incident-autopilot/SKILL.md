# Incident Autopilot

Establish timeline and blast radius. Stabilize safely before speculative fixes. Preserve logs and reproduction evidence. Separate containment from root-cause remediation. Verify recovery with user-impact checks and add regression/monitoring improvements. Never delete evidence or hide uncertainty.
