# Customer Discovery

Define the ideal customer profile and the exact problem solved. For every prospect, collect public evidence of the problem, relevant technical context, decision-maker path, urgency signals, and disqualifiers. Score confidence separately from opportunity size. Never fabricate a company need, contact, response, or buying intent.
