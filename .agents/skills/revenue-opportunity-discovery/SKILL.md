# Revenue Opportunity Discovery

Find opportunities from public, lawful sources. For every candidate:

1. Identify the underlying customer problem or economic event.
2. Capture the source and evidence.
3. Identify the payer and exact monetization mechanism.
4. Check public terms, eligibility, payout mechanics, and material restrictions.
5. Reject self-referrals, fake accounts, spam, cookie stuffing, deceptive claims, and guaranteed-income claims.
6. Score revenue potential, commission or willingness to pay, demand, competition, automation potential, longevity, payout feasibility, and risk separately.
7. Produce a machine-readable candidate for the Revenue Engine.

Never treat credits, traffic, impressions, or a referral click as cash. A revenue claim requires a confirmed provider event.

When evidence is incomplete, return `needs_verification` rather than guessing.
