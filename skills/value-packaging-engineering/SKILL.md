# Value Packaging & Perceived-Value Engineering

## Purpose
Increase a product's perceived value through truthful presentation, brand coherence, story, packaging, photography/video, and unboxing experience without misrepresenting the underlying product.

## Contract
product facts → audience/occasion → positioning → brand/name → packaging concept → story → visual assets → unboxing journey → offer/page → A/B test → measure willingness-to-buy/conversion → learn.

## Rules
- Packaging is a value signal, not permission to make false quality claims.
- Preserve Product DNA: product identity, material, color, dimensions, ingredients/specifications, safety information and other protected attributes.
- Separate real added value from cosmetic signaling; disclose material changes and sustainability claims.
- Prefer evidence from controlled experiments over assumed price multipliers.
- Test packaging, story, photography and offer separately where practical to identify causal lift.
- Track incremental production/packaging cost against incremental revenue and margin.

## EASY integration
Use Product DNA + Product Integrity as the source of truth. Generate:
- brand/name directions
- packaging concepts
- product story
- product photography/creative briefs
- unboxing sequence
- product-page copy and creative variants
Then validate that generated visuals and copy do not alter protected product attributes.

## MONY integration
Package this as a service/revenue capability for sellers and small brands:
- low-cost product → premium presentation package
- before/after creative variants
- landing/product page
- short-form video/unboxing creative
- measurable offer test
Revenue claims require actual sales/conversion evidence, not visual quality alone.

## Evidence
Record baseline offer/creative, variant, traffic/exposure, conversion or purchase outcome, average order value where available, incremental cost, margin impact, and test limitations.
