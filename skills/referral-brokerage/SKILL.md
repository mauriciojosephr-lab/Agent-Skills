# Referral Brokerage

## Purpose

Convert verified demand into commissionable introductions without requiring delivery of the underlying service.

## Contract

Opportunity → verify buyer need → verify provider capacity → agree referral terms → register lead → make introduction → track outcome → verify collected payment → reconcile commission → learn.

## Guardrails

- Never promise a provider's capabilities or price without confirmation.
- Get commission terms and attribution/protection window in writing before the introduction.
- Track the referred lead and the exact provider, date, service and agreed commission.
- Commission is recognized only after the contractual earning trigger is satisfied, preferably collected customer payment when agreed.
- Respect privacy, consent, anti-spam rules, platform terms, licensing and local commercial/tax requirements.
- No fake leads, self-referrals, undisclosed conflicts, spam or impersonation.

## Provider selection

Score operationally, not politically:
- verified service capability
- response time
- capacity
- price fit
- delivery history/evidence
- geography/language fit
- commission terms
- attribution reliability

## MONY integration

Use brokerage as a revenue path alongside affiliate revenue:
- discover buyers with a concrete need
- discover providers willing to pay for qualified introductions
- match only when both sides are verified
- package the introduction
- record attribution evidence
- reconcile paid outcomes

The system should prefer a small number of high-confidence matches over bulk unverified lead spam.

## Evidence

Store: buyer need, provider identity, consent/permission where applicable, lead ID, referral timestamp, provider acceptance, deal status, payment evidence when available, commission calculation, payout status, and failure reason.

Do not claim revenue from a lead, meeting, proposal or signed contract unless the agreed commission trigger has actually occurred.
