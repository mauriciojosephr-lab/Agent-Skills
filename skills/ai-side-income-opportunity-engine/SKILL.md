# AI Side-Income Opportunity Engine

## Purpose
Turn a large idea catalog into a repeatable, evidence-driven revenue-testing pipeline for MONY. The engine does not assume an idea is profitable because it is popular; it verifies demand, buyer, deliverable, acquisition path, unit economics, fulfillment feasibility, and revenue evidence.

## Pipeline
idea catalog → normalize → capability mapping → demand signal → buyer/use case → offer definition → fulfillment path → cost/margin model → acquisition test → outcome evidence → scale/kill

## Opportunity contract
Every opportunity should record:
- ideaId and category
- buyer/persona
- concrete deliverable/outcome
- acquisition channel
- required capabilities/tools
- provider dependencies
- estimated fulfillment effort
- direct variable cost
- target price/range
- commission/referral option where applicable
- evidence level
- blockers and compliance constraints
- experiment definition and success metric

## Evidence levels
- DISCOVERY: idea only; no market evidence.
- SIGNAL: external demand/search/job/marketplace signal exists.
- OFFER_READY: buyer, deliverable, fulfillment and economics are defined.
- TESTED: real outreach/traffic produced measurable buyer behavior.
- REVENUE_VERIFIED: collected payment or provider-confirmed commission evidence exists.

Never convert one evidence level into a stronger one without new evidence.

## Routing
Prefer opportunities that reuse existing MONY/EASY/Elite capabilities:
- AI video production
- AI automation/integration
- value packaging and product presentation
- referral brokerage
- multimodal generation
- data/document automation
- analytics and conversion optimization

Use provider-independent adapters and fail closed on missing credentials, quotas, authentication, licensing, or output validation.

## Experiment design
For each candidate, produce one small paid outcome that can be delivered quickly. Measure:
- qualified leads
- replies
- calls/meetings
- paid orders
- collected revenue
- fulfillment time
- variable cost
- contribution margin
- repeat/upsell signal

Do not use popularity, subjective rankings, or model confidence as proof of profitability.

## Safety and integrity
- No fake leads, fabricated testimonials, deceptive claims, spam, or undisclosed conflicts.
- Preserve customer/product facts and protected attributes.
- For regulated domains, require appropriate human/professional review.
- Never bypass authentication, quotas, rate limits, CAPTCHA/MFA, or platform protections.
- Do not store secrets or live affiliate URLs in source.

## MONY integration
MONY should treat this as an opportunity catalog and execution contract, not as 50 independent businesses. Cluster similar ideas into reusable production lanes and reuse verified capabilities.

## Acceptance evidence
A completed opportunity test must include the opportunity contract, acquisition evidence, fulfillment artifact, economics, and an explicit outcome status. Revenue is recognized only from actual collected payment or provider-confirmed commission evidence.
