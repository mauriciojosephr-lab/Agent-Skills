## Hard filters

- No fake country, address, identity, or tax information.
- No VPN/proxy used to misrepresent eligibility.
- No self-referral.
- No paid opportunity requiring upfront money unless independently verified and explicitly approved.
- No projected income counted as revenue.
- A payout is considered real only when the provider confirms the earning and the withdrawal path is available.
