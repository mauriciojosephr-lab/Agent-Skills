# Army-14 Evolution Laboratory

The Evolution Laboratory is the evidence layer for measurable soldier improvement.

## Contract
- reproducible baseline
- identical metric contract before and after
- benchmark measurements
- adversarial verification
- regression verification
- Elite verification
- durable experiment record

## Labs
Baseline, Benchmark, Evolution, Adversarial, Performance, Recovery, Architecture, Parallelism, Memory/Learning, Elite Verification, Regression.

## Promotion
Promote only when safety evidence passes, success rate or latency materially improves, retry/rework do not regress, and the candidate is verified.

Failed experiments remain recorded because rejection is learning.

Live provider capability must still be proven with live provider evidence; simulated tests do not count as live revenue or external execution.
